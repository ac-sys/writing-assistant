Technology is vital in higher education. Students use many digital tools to improve their learning. Lecturers use online platforms to help them learn together. Technology also needs strong digital literacy skills. A 2022 survey of 1,200 students at Chinhoyi University of Technology found that 68% used e-learning platforms weekly (Moyo, 2023).

Mobile learning is a big step forward for rural students. It gives them access to learning materials they could not get before. It lets them learn at their own speed. And it helps teachers teach more people outside the classroom. As Banda et al. (2021) note, "connectivity remains the single biggest barrier to equitable access." To sum up, technology shows how innovation can change education.
