Technology is key in higher ed. Students use lots of digital stuff to learn better. Teachers use online places to help everyone work together. And tech needs good digital skills. A 2022 survey of 1,200 students at Chinhoyi University of Technology found that 68% used e-learning platforms weekly (Moyo, 2023).

Mobile learning is a big help for children in places far away. It lets them learn new stuff without leaving home. It also helps teachers teach everyone, not just in school buildings. As Banda et al. (2021) note, "connectivity remains the single biggest barrier to equitable access." To sum up, technology shows how innovation can change education.
