In today's fast-paced world, technology plays a crucial role in higher education. It is important to note that students utilize a myriad of digital tools in order to enhance their learning outcomes. Furthermore, lecturers leverage online platforms to foster collaboration among learners. Moreover, the integration of technology underscores the need for robust digital literacy skills. A 2022 survey of 1,200 students at Chinhoyi University of Technology found that 68% used e-learning platforms weekly (Moyo, 2023).

Additionally, mobile learning serves as a game-changer for students in rural areas. It provides access to learning materials that were previously unavailable. It allows students to study at their own pace. It enables lecturers to reach learners beyond the classroom. As Banda et al. (2021) note, "connectivity remains the single biggest barrier to equitable access." In conclusion, technology is a testament to the transformative potential of innovation in the realm of education.
