Technology is vital in higher education. Students use many digital tools to learn better. Teachers use online places to help everyone work together. Technology also means people need good technology skills. A 2022 survey of 1,200 students at Chinhoyi University of Technology found that 68% used e-learning platforms weekly (Moyo, 2023).

Mobile learning is a major shift for rural students. It brings learning resources they could not get before. It lets them learn at their own time. It helps teachers teach to places outside the school. As Banda et al. (2021) note, "connectivity remains the single biggest barrier to equitable access." Technology shows how innovation can change education.
