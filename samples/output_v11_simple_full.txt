Technology is key in higher ed. Students use lots of digital stuff to learn better. Teachers use online places to help everyone work together. And it needs good digital skills. A 2022 survey of 1,200 students at Chinhoyi University of Technology found that 68% used e-learning platforms weekly (Moyo, 2023).

Mobile learning is a big help for children in places far away. It lets them learn new stuff when they want to. And teachers can teach everyone, not just in school buildings. As Banda et al. (2021) note, "connectivity remains the single biggest barrier to equitable access." Technology shows how great ideas can change learning.
