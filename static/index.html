    Local model (offline, free)

    Downloaded once on first use, then works without internet. The 0.5B and 360M models are 3-5× faster on CPU; Dynamic INT8 cuts memory in half and accelerates CPU inference.

    Optional: online model (higher quality)

    Any OpenAI-compatible service: OpenAI https://api.openai.com/v1, xAI Grok https://api.x.ai/v1, Groq https://api.groq.com/openai/v1, or a local server such as Ollama http://localhost:11434/v1. Your text is sent to that service. If it fails, the local model is used.

    This app is an editing aid for clarity, tone and style. It does not score text for AI detection and has no detector-evasion features. If your institution requires you to disclose AI or editing-tool use, do so.
